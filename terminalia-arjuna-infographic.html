अर्जुन

Terminalia arjuna

Arjuna  ·  Kahu, Arjun sadada, Vellai Marutha
FamilyCombretaceae
Part usedBark (Twak)
HabitatRiverbanks, moist forests
CharacterSmooth grey, flakes off
Pañcavidha profile
1RASAKashaya — astringent
2GUṆALaghu, Rūkṣa — light, dry
3VĪRYAŚīta — cooling potency
4VIPĀKAKaṭu — pungent post-digestive taste
5PRABHĀVAHṛdya — special, non-inferable cardiotonic action
Karma
Hṛdya Vraṇaropaṇa Bhagna-sandhānakara Raktapittahara Medohara Kapha-Pittahara
Clinical indications
Classically reached for in Hṛdroga (cardiac disorders), Vraṇa (non-healing wounds) and Bhagna (fracture healing), Prameha, and Raktapitta. Contemporary pharmacology attributes cardioprotective and antioxidant activity chiefly to arjunolic acid and related bark triterpenoid glycosides.
